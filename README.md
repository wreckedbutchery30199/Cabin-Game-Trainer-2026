## 🎮 About the Game

the cabin game is a first-person psychological horror and card-based action game developed and published by Twisted Corridor. It entered Steam Early Access on August 27, 2026. Designed for one to four players, it combines online co-op, exploration, procedural room layouts, supernatural threats, and card-driven puzzles.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **the cabin game**. This trainer provides configurable assistance for card management, investigation, exploration, survival, and cabin progression. It is designed around the game's core loop: playing cards at the table, building the cabin, searching rooms for clues, managing the clock, and avoiding or surviving monster encounters.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the cabin freely, study its card-driven systems, practice investigation routes, reduce the pressure of monster encounters, or experiment with room layouts and escape strategies.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **No Collision** — Allows the player to move through selected environmental obstacles when navigating cramped rooms or escaping a pursuit.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement options to search connected rooms more efficiently and escape monster chases without changing the cabin's layout. Speed Control is useful for routine exploration, while No Collision should be reserved for getting unstuck or compatibility difficult geometry. Disable movement assistance before entering scripted events or multiplayer sessions if unusual behavior occurs.

### Tools & Inventory

The game's primary run resources are the cards dealt at the table, investigation information, and candy used at the Boo Factory. Unlimited Card Plays is intended for testing card functions and experimenting with room placement. Infinite Candy affects purchases and customization rather than the core investigation objective.

### World & Environment

Cards shape the cabin by placing rooms, triggering events, revealing clues, and changing environmental conditions. Freeze Clock and Reveal Investigation Clues make it easier to examine the procedurally arranged cabin and understand how early room choices influence later objectives. Keep the trainer disabled while learning the normal progression of a run.

### Combat & Weapons

The cabin game focuses on survival, investigation, and evasion rather than conventional weapon-based combat. Monsters create pressure through pursuit and environmental encounters. Invulnerability protects the player from damage, while Freeze Monsters temporarily removes pursuit pressure so players can inspect rooms, resolve cards, and reach the correct escape area.
